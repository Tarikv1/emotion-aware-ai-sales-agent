# RESP-006 German Human Listening Decision

Date: 2026-05-08

Reviewer: Tarik

## Decision

Neither RESP-006 German variant is accepted yet.

Both are close enough to keep, but both need a pacing revision before they can feed the voice-personality selector.

## Variant Notes

### old_plain_guarded

- Starts a bit too fast.
- Then becomes a bit too slow.
- Main issue: uneven pacing across the answer.

### new_shaped_runtime

- Starts strong.
- Then becomes a bit too fast later in the answer.
- Main issue: late-answer pacing accelerates too much.

## Product Implication

The German runtime needs a narrow pacing-stability pass before the project defines shared English/German voice-personality profiles.

The next German test should keep the same question and answer content, then change only pacing-related delivery surfaces so the result is comparable to RESP-006.

## Boundary

- This is a human listening preference and pacing decision, not a production-wide quality claim.
- No claim is made that either German voice style is ready for all campaigns, all providers, all voice IDs, or real leads.
- No private customer data, voice cloning, transcription, or customer audio upload was involved in the recorded RESP-006 decision.
