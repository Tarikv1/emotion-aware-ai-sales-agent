# Universal Persuasion Strategy KB

## Purpose

This KB defines ethical consultative persuasion strategies that work across campaigns. Every strategy must use approved campaign facts and respect capability boundaries, side-effect boundaries, and stop requests.

## Strategies

### Problem Clarification

Ask one question that helps the buyer define the current pain, friction, cost, risk, or missed opportunity.

Use when: buyer is vague but not refusing.

Do not use when: buyer asked to stop.

### Consequence Framing

Explain the practical cost of the current problem in cautious, non-alarmist terms.

Use when: the buyer has acknowledged a problem.

Do not use when: the consequence is not supported by adapter facts.

### Pain-to-Value Bridge

Connect the buyer's stated pain to an approved outcome.

Use when: a pain signal exists and the adapter maps it to value.

Do not use when: the agent is guessing.

### Proof-Based Reassurance

Use approved proof points, examples, or case studies to reduce uncertainty.

Use when: buyer asks whether it works or whether the company is credible.

Do not use when: proof_points are missing.

### Risk Reversal

Explain approved trial, demo, refund, cancellation, or no-commitment policy.

Use when: buyer fears commitment.

Do not use when: no approved policy exists.

### Comparative Clarity

Compare against alternatives using approved comparison_rules.

Use when: buyer mentions a competitor or alternative.

Do not use when: comparison would require unsupported claims.

### Next-Step Simplification

Reduce friction by explaining the smallest approved next step.

Use when: buyer shows mild interest or uncertainty.

Do not use when: next step is unclear or unavailable.

### Authority Transfer

Route to a human or specialist only when approved.

Use when: buyer asks for legal, financial, technical, procurement, or account-specific detail beyond the agent boundary.

Do not use when: no handoff policy exists.

### Disqualification Honesty

Tell the buyer the offer is not a fit when required by disqualification_rules.

Use when: buyer is outside target or asks for unsupported outcomes.

Do not use when: a normal objection can be answered safely.

### Trust Repair

Clarify identity, reason for contact, limits, and exit path.

Use when: buyer suspects deception, spam, or hidden authority.

Do not use by making unverifiable authority claims.

## Strategy Selection Rules

- Strategy selection follows buyer state, not script order.
- No fake guarantees.
- No fake authority.
- No pressure after refusal.
- No bracketed/internal labels in buyer-facing language.
