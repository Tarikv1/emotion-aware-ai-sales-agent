# Website Offer And Service Levels

## Core Offer

[AGENCY_NAME] builds modern, mobile-friendly websites for local businesses. The point is to make it easier for visitors to understand the business, trust it, and take action: call, book, request a quote, order, or visit.

The low-risk first step is a free homepage mockup/demo. The buyer can review a concrete direction before discussing a full site.

## First Conversion Goal

Ask for permission to prepare a free homepage mockup/demo or agree that a short website review call would be useful.

Preferred close:
"Would it be useful if we prepared a free homepage mockup so you can see what this could look like for your business?"

## Service Levels

| Level | Positioning | Typical fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter Website | Simple 1-3 page local business site with mobile-first layout, trust sections, contact path, and service or menu details. | New or small local business that needs a credible first site. |
| Growth Website | 5-7 page site with lead form, service pages, local SEO basics, reviews/photos, and clear action paths. | Business with multiple services or a weak current site. |
| Premium Website | Advanced booking, gallery, ecommerce, member area, or custom integrations. | Business with higher-value services or stronger operations. |
| Monthly Care Plan | Hosting, edits, maintenance, analytics/reporting, backups, and ongoing content support. | Business that wants the site maintained instead of self-managed. |

## Sell Outcomes, Not Features

Use outcome language:

- "Make it easier for phone visitors to call."
- "Help people see services, photos, reviews, location, and next steps fast."
- "Turn Google and social visitors into bookings, quote requests, orders, or walk-ins."
- "Give the owner something visual to review before committing."

Avoid feature dumping:

- Do not lead with frameworks, plugins, hosting details, or design jargon.
- Do not promise leads, rankings, sales, revenue, or conversion results.
- Do not use fake scarcity or fake urgency.

## Price Questions

If asked for exact price:
"The right price depends on scope, pages, features, and ongoing support. The simple first step is the free homepage mockup, so you can judge whether the direction is useful before discussing a full site."

If pushed for a range:
"[AGENCY_NAME] would confirm scope before quoting. I do not want to guess and mislead you. The mockup is the best way to see whether a conversation is worth having."
