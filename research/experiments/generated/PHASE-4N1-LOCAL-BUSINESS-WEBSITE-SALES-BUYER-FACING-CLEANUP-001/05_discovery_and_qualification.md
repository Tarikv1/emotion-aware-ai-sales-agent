# Discovery And Qualification

## Goal

Ask enough to decide whether a free homepage mockup/demo or short website review call is useful. Do not interrogate the buyer.

## Opening

"Hi, this is [NAME] from [AGENCY_NAME]. I'll keep it quick. We build websites for local businesses that help turn visitors into calls, bookings, or quote requests. Would seeing a free homepage mockup for your business be useful?"

## Qualification Questions

Use one or two, then move to the mockup close.

- "Do most new customers find you through Google, referrals, social media, or walk-ins?"
- "Do you already have a website, or are people mostly using social pages?"
- "If someone visits on a phone, can they call, book, order, or request a quote quickly?"
- "Which service or offer would you most want new customers to notice first?"
- "Are reviews, photos, services, menu, or pricing easy to find online?"
- "Who usually decides on website or marketing changes?"

## Pain Signals

Listen for:

- no website,
- outdated site,
- not mobile-friendly,
- no booking/request path,
- poor trust/credibility,
- missing service pages,
- weak local SEO basics,
- no photos/gallery,
- no reviews/testimonials shown,
- no menu/listings/service pricing.

## Qualification Scores

High fit:

- buyer has no website or a visibly weak one,
- buyer cares about calls, bookings, quote requests, orders, or walk-ins,
- owner or decision-maker is reachable,
- buyer is open to seeing a mockup.

Medium fit:

- buyer already has a website but names a gap,
- buyer relies on Instagram/Facebook but admits conversion friction,
- buyer needs partner review.

Low fit:

- buyer has a strong website and no problem,
- no online acquisition need,
- no desire to review anything,
- wrong person or wrong number,
- explicit stop request.

## Qualification Close

"Based on that, the most useful low-risk step would be a free homepage mockup focused on the action you want customers to take. Would it be useful if we prepared that for review?"

## Disqualification Line

"That sounds like this probably is not a priority right now. I will leave it there. Thanks for your time."
