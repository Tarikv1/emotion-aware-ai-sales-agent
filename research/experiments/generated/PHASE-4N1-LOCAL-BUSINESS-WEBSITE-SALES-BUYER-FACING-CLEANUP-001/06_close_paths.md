# Close Paths

The first conversion goal is permission, not payment.

## Free mockup yes

Use when the buyer has a website problem, is curious but cautious, asks for info, or already uses social but lacks a stable conversion path.

Buyer-facing line:
"I can prepare a free homepage mockup around that. Who would be the best person to review it?"

## Review call yes

Use when the buyer wants to talk through an existing site, understand what would change, or compare options.

Buyer-facing line:
"A short website review call may be easier if you want to talk through the current site. Who should be involved in that review?"

## Qualified follow-up

Use when the buyer gives permission but no next action can be completed inside the conversation.

Buyer-facing line:
"The next step would be for [AGENCY_NAME] to prepare the mockup for review. Who would normally review something like that?"

## Send info / send mockup

Use when the buyer says "send me info" or asks for something to review.

Buyer-facing line:
"A custom mockup is usually more useful than generic information. What should it focus on first: calls, bookings, quote requests, or trust?"

## Wrong person / referral to decision maker

Use when the person is not responsible for the website or marketing decision.

Buyer-facing line:
"Thanks for clarifying. Who is the best person to review website changes or a free mockup?"

## Disqualified

Use when the buyer has no need, no interest, requires guarantees, or already has a strong site with no problem to solve.

Buyer-facing line:
"It sounds like this is not a fit right now. I will leave it there."

## Stop respected

Use when the buyer asks the agent to stop, says not to call, or clearly refuses further contact.

Buyer-facing line:
"Understood. I will stop. Thanks for your time."
