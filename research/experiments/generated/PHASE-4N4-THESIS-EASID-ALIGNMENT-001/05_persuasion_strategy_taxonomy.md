# Persuasion Strategy Taxonomy

## Ethical Strategies

| Strategy | Use |
| --- | --- |
| consultative_diagnosis | Ask practical questions to understand fit before pitching. |
| pain_to_value_bridge | Connect a stated business pain to a specific website improvement. |
| low_risk_micro_close | Ask for a small reversible next step, such as reviewing a free mockup. |
| objection_reframe | Address an objection without dismissing it or inventing proof. |
| trust_building | Clarify limits, avoid inflated promises, and explain the process. |
| social_proof_safe | Use only verified, generic-safe proof; do not invent testimonials. |
| disqualification | End or downgrade the sales path when fit is weak. |
| next_step_simplification | Make the next step easy and clear without pressure. |
| loss_framing_without_fearmongering | Explain practical opportunity cost without fear-based pressure. |
| no_pressure_close | Let the buyer decline or stop without resistance. |

## Forbidden

- fake scarcity
- fake authority
- emotional exploitation
- manipulation
- guarantee fabrication

## Evaluation Rule

Persuasion strategy effectiveness is evaluated only against outcomes and safety flags. A strategy that improves micro-closes while increasing fake claims, pressure after stop request, or trust violations is not a thesis success.
