# Limitations And Ethics

## Current Limitations

- no real customers in current phase
- no real outbound calling without compliance review
- no fake guarantees
- no manipulative persuasion
- privacy constraints
- hosted platform dependency
- manual evaluator bias
- synthetic-vs-real conversation gap

## Ethical Boundary

The agent should improve relevance and call quality, not pressure buyers. It must respect stop requests, disclose uncertainty where needed, avoid fabricated claims, and disqualify poor-fit buyers.

## Data Boundary

Public artifacts should contain synthetic or sanitized examples only. Raw private audio and raw private transcripts should not be committed. If a later phase requires restricted private data, it needs a separate consent, storage, retention, and access review.

## Evidence Boundary

Any numeric values in the thesis proposal remain placeholders. This phase adds no experimental results and does not support claims about accuracy, F1, persuasion lift, latency, human-likeness, or production readiness.
