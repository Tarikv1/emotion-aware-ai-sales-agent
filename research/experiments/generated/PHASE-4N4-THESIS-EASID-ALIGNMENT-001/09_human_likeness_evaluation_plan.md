# Human-Likeness Evaluation Plan

## Rubric Dimensions

Human-likeness should be scored manually until a validated automatic metric exists. The core dimensions are:

- spoken naturalness
- concise call control
- trust
- buyer-state adaptation
- no robotic/internal wording

## Evaluation Inputs

Use sanitized transcripts or controlled test-call transcripts. If audio is available under privacy rules, evaluators may also rate pacing, pauses, interruptions, and natural turn-taking.

## Scoring Boundary

Human-likeness is not the same as sounding friendly. A human-like agent must respect stop requests, avoid fake guarantees, handle wrong-person cases, and stop selling when disqualification is the correct outcome.

## Evidence Needed

The thesis can claim human-likeness only after evaluator scores exist and the scoring instructions are reproducible.
