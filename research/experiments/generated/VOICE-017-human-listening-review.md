# VOICE-017 Human Listening Review

## Result

The project owner listened to the VOICE-017 live ElevenLabs A/B outputs and strongly preferred the prosody variants over the plain variants.

## Claim Scope

Allowed claim:

```text
In the VOICE-017 two-case ElevenLabs live A/B run, prosody-shaped speech was strongly preferred over plain speech by the human listener.
```

Do not claim yet:

- prosody is always better across all providers
- prosody is always better across all voices
- prosody is always better across all scripts or product categories
- customers will prefer prosody in live sales calls

## Evidence

- Provider: ElevenLabs
- Cases: 2
- Languages: 1 English, 1 German
- Variants: plain vs prosody
- Audio files created: 4
- Fallbacks: 0
- Customer audio uploaded: false
- Voice cloning used: false

## Interpretation

This is enough to move from "no quality claim before listening" to a bounded positive result for VOICE-017.

The useful product lesson is that the VOICE-015 and VOICE-016 prosody stack is directionally valuable and should be kept. The next useful evaluation question is not whether prosody matters at all, but how much prosody is optimal before it starts sounding unnatural or distracting.
