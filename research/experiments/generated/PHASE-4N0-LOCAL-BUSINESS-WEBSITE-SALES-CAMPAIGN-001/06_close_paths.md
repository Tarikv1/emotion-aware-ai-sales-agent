# Close Paths

The first conversion goal is permission, not payment.

## Preferred Close

"Would it be useful if we sent over a free homepage mockup so you can see what this could look like for your business?"

Use when:

- the buyer has a website problem,
- the buyer is curious but cautious,
- the buyer asks for info,
- the buyer already uses social but lacks a stable conversion path.

## Secondary Close

"Who would be the best person to review the mockup?"

Use when:

- the buyer mentions a partner, manager, owner, marketing person, or office manager,
- the buyer is not the decision-maker,
- the buyer says to send information.

## Tertiary Close

"What email should we send it to?"

Use when:

- permission is clear,
- the buyer asks for the mockup or info,
- the buyer wants partner review.

## Review/Demo Call Close

"Would a short website review/demo call be easier than reviewing a mockup by email?"

Use when:

- the buyer wants to talk through problems,
- the buyer has an existing site and wants critique,
- the buyer asks what would change.

## Qualified Follow-Up Close

"I cannot book anything from here, but the next safe step would be for [AGENCY_NAME] to follow up manually with the mockup or review details."

Use when:

- the buyer gives permission but no side-effect tool exists,
- the agent must avoid pretending to send email, book calendar events, or write CRM notes.

## Disqualification Close

"It sounds like this is not a fit right now. I will leave it there."

Use when:

- owner clearly uninterested,
- no online acquisition need,
- no budget and no desire,
- wrong person or number,
- already has a strong site and no problem,
- compliance concern,
- asks to stop.
