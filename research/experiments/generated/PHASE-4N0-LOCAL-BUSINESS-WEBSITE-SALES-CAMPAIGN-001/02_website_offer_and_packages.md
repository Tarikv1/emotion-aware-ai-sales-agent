# Website Offer And Packages

## Primary Offer

"We build modern, mobile-friendly websites for local businesses that help turn visitors into calls, bookings, quote requests, or walk-ins. We can create a free homepage/demo mockup first, and if you like it, we can talk about finishing the full site."

## First Conversion Goal

The first conversion goal is permission to send a free custom homepage/mockup or agreement to a short website review/demo call.

Preferred close:
"Would it be useful if we sent over a free homepage mockup so you can see what this could look like for your business?"

## Internal Test Pricing

Pricing below is internal testing placeholder - replace before real use.

These are not final business prices and must not be presented as final pricing.

| Package | Internal test positioning | Typical fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter Website | Simple 1-3 page local business site with mobile-first layout, trust sections, contact path, and basic service/menu info. | New or small local business that needs a credible first website. |
| Growth Website | 5-7 page site with lead form, service pages, local SEO basics, reviews/photos, and clear conversion paths. | Business with multiple services or a weak current website. |
| Premium Website | Advanced booking, gallery, ecommerce, member area, or custom integrations. | Business with stronger operations or higher-value services. |
| Monthly Care Plan | Hosting, edits, maintenance, analytics/reporting, backups, and ongoing content support. | Business that wants the site maintained instead of self-managed. |

## Sell Outcomes, Not Features

Use outcome language:

- "Make it easier for phone visitors to call."
- "Help people see services, photos, reviews, and location fast."
- "Turn Google and social visitors into bookings, quote requests, orders, or walk-ins."
- "Give the owner something visual to review before committing."

Avoid feature dumping:

- Do not lead with frameworks, plugins, hosting details, or design jargon.
- Do not promise leads, rankings, sales, revenue, or conversion results.
- Do not use fake scarcity or fake urgency.

## Safe Pricing Language

If asked for exact price:

"For this internal test, the package levels are only placeholders. In a real sales call, [AGENCY_NAME] would confirm scope and pricing before presenting a final quote. The low-risk first step is the free homepage mockup, so you can judge whether the direction is useful before discussing a full site."
