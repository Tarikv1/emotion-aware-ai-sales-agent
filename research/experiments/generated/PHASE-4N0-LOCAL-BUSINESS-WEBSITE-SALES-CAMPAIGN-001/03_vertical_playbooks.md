# Vertical Playbooks

Use each playbook to move from likely pain to a specific free mockup angle. Keep the first call short and close for permission.

## restaurants

- Likely pain: outdated menu, weak mobile view, no reservation/order path, unclear location or hours.
- Business value: turn Google, map, and social visitors into calls, bookings, orders, or walk-ins.
- Example opening angle: "A lot of restaurants lose mobile visitors when the menu, booking, or location path is hard to use."
- Qualification questions: "Can guests book or order from the site on a phone?" "Is the menu current online?"
- Demo/mockup angle: homepage with menu highlights, booking/order CTA, reviews, location, hours, and photos.
- Objections: Instagram-only, existing menu PDF, busy manager.
- Close line: "I can send a mockup with menu, booking, and location sections."

## cafes

- Likely pain: visitors cannot quickly confirm hours, location, menu, photos, or catering/private event options.
- Business value: make social and map traffic more likely to become walk-ins, calls, or catering requests.
- Example opening angle: "For cafes, the website mainly needs to make the place feel worth visiting and easy to find."
- Qualification questions: "Do people usually check your hours and menu before visiting?" "Do you promote catering, events, or seasonal items?"
- Demo/mockup angle: warm mobile homepage with menu highlights, photos, hours, map, reviews, and order/catering CTA.
- Objections: social page is enough, menu changes often.
- Close line: "I can send a mockup that makes the cafe easy to trust, find, and visit from a phone."
