# Thesis Relevance Note

4O4 adds lead context as a missing layer in outbound sales-agent behavior.

The problem is not only what Emma says. It is what Emma should already know before a call. Asking a known prospect for the business name or business type makes the agent sound unprepared and breaks the intended outbound use case.

The useful thesis distinction is:

- discovery from zero belongs to inbound or unknown contexts
- outbound prospect calls should confirm known context, hedge uncertain context, and ask only for missing details

This checkpoint stays non-live. It adds schema, templates, examples, and tests, but does not scrape leads, call providers, enable real outbound calls, or enable email, calendar, CRM, payment, or account actions.
