# Vertical Playbooks

Use each playbook to move from likely pain to a specific free mockup angle. Keep the call short and close for permission.

## restaurants

- Likely pain: outdated menu, weak mobile view, no reservation/order path, unclear location or hours.
- Business value: turn Google, map, and social visitors into calls, bookings, orders, or walk-ins.
- Opening angle: "Many restaurants lose mobile visitors when the menu, booking, or location path is hard to use."
- Qualification questions: "Can guests book or order from the site on a phone?" "Is the menu current online?"
- Mockup angle: homepage with menu highlights, booking/order action, reviews, location, hours, and photos.
- Close line: "I can prepare a mockup with menu, booking, and location sections. Would that be worth reviewing?"
