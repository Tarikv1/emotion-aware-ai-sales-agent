# RESP-005 Human Listening Decision

Date: 2026-05-08

Reviewer: Tarik

## Decision

Both RESP-005 variants are accepted as useful voice personality directions. This is no longer a simple old-runtime versus new-runtime quality contest.

The deciding factor is now listener and campaign preference.

## Variant Notes

### old_plain_guarded

- Accepted as a natural, real, laid-back salesperson direction.
- Keep as a candidate personality lane instead of discarding it as "older."

### new_shaped_runtime

- Accepted as a more serious and lower-energy direction.
- Keep as a candidate personality lane instead of treating it as the universal replacement.

## Product Implication

The next runtime step should expose bounded selectable voice-personality profiles rather than keep tuning toward one universal voice. Future evaluations should ask which personality fits the lead, campaign, and product category better.

## Boundary

- This is a human listening preference decision, not a production-wide quality claim.
- No claim is made that either voice is ready for all campaigns, all providers, all voice IDs, or real leads.
- No private customer data, voice cloning, transcription, or customer audio upload was involved in the recorded RESP-005 decision.
