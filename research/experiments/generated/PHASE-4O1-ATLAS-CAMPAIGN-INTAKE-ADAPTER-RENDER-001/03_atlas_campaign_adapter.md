## Normalized Product Facts

Atlas Web Studio sells local business website design. The safe offer is a free homepage mockup/demo that lets the buyer judge a direction before a paid project.

Approved product facts:

- Websites help local visitors understand, trust, and act.
- The relevant action depends on vertical: call, book, order, request a quote, or visit.
- The mockup/demo is the first step and does not create a paid obligation.
- Pricing uses internal test ranges only and must be replaced before real commercial use.

## Approved Claims

- A clearer website can make the business easier to understand and contact.
- Mobile-first action paths can reduce friction for calls, bookings, orders, visits, or quote requests.
- Reviews, photos, service/menu details, hours, location, and trust sections help buyers decide.
- A mockup/demo gives a concrete visual comparison before discussing a paid site.

## Forbidden Claims

- Guaranteed leads, revenue, calls, bookings, sales, walk-ins, or rankings.
- Official platform authority or third-party partnership.
- Final public pricing or final contract terms from internal test ranges.
- Any fake side effect.

## Campaign-Specific Fixes From Dashboard Failures

- Beauty salon Instagram objection: Instagram gets attention; website turns interest into booking through services, price/service types, reviews, photos, and one booking path.
- Plumber emergency call value: focus on mobile click-to-call, emergency service section, service area, reviews/trust, and quote/call path. Do not imply CRM, secure lead handling, email, or lead-processing tools.
- Restaurant no website: Google and social help people find the restaurant; website helps them act through menu, hours, location, photos, reviews, and booking/order/call path.
- Strong current website: disqualify politely if there is no problem. If buyer re-engages, answer briefly and ask permission before offering a mockup again.
- Pricing: give the approved range instead of evasive "it depends" wording.
