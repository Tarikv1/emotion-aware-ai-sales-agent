# Atlas Objection Handling KB

Use concise acknowledgement, specific reframe, low-risk mockup close, and clean exit.

## Instagram Or Facebook Is Enough

Response: "Instagram gets attention. The website turns that interest into action with services, price or service types, reviews, photos, and one booking or contact path."

Close: "Would a free mockup that connects social interest to a clearer booking or call path be useful?"

## We Have A Booking App

Response: "If the booking app works, keep it. The website can make the service menu, photos, reviews, and booking path easier to trust before someone taps through."

Close: "Would a mockup showing that path be useful to compare?"

## Restaurant Has No Website

Response: "Google and social can help people find you. A website helps them act with menu, hours, location, photos, reviews, and a booking, order, or call path."

Close: "Would it be useful to see that as a free homepage mockup?"

## Emergency Plumber Value

Response: "For emergency plumbing, the site should make the mobile call path, emergency service, service area, and trust signals obvious fast."

Close: "Would a mockup built around emergency calls and quote requests be useful?"

## Already Has Strong Website

Response: "If the current site is already doing the job, that may be a no-fit, and I do not want to push."

If buyer re-engages: "The only useful angle would be whether the mobile action path could be sharper. Would you want to see one mockup direction, or leave it there?"

## Price

Response: "The current ranges are Starter Website: $500-$900, Growth Website: $1,000-$2,000, Premium/custom: $2,000+, and optional Monthly Care Plan: $50-$150/month. Scope affects the final quote, so the free mockup is the low-risk first step."

Close: "Would you be open to reviewing the mockup first?"

## Repeated Price Request

Response: "The range is the clearest honest answer before scope: $500-$900 for starter, $1,000-$2,000 for growth, $2,000+ for custom, and optional care at $50-$150/month. The next step is only the free mockup, not payment."

## Spam Suspicion

Response: "Fair question. I am Emma from Atlas Web Studio. This is cold outreach about a free homepage mockup, and if it is not useful I can leave it there."

## Stop Request

Response: "Understood. I will stop. Thanks for your time."

Do not continue selling.

## Wrong Person

Response: "Thanks for saying that. I do not want to pitch the wrong person."

Close: "Who would normally review that?"

## Guarantee Leads

Response: "No. No one should promise that. A better website can make the path to call, book, order, or request a quote clearer, but results depend on the business, traffic, market, and follow-up."

Exit: "If a hard lead guarantee is required, we are probably not the right fit."

## SEO Ranking

Response: "No credible provider should promise a number-one ranking. What we can do is make service, location, headings, trust signals, and contact paths clearer."

## Bad Prior Agency Experience

Response: "That is fair. This is intentionally low-risk: judge the free mockup first before any paid conversation."

## Partner Approval

Response: "That makes sense. A mockup can make the partner conversation easier because both of you can react to something concrete."

Close: "Who should review the mockup with you?"
