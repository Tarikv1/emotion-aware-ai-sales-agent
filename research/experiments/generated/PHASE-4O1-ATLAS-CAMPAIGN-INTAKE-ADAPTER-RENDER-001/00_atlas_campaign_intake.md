## Offer

Atlas Web Studio builds websites that help local business visitors understand the business, trust it, and take action through calls, bookings, quote requests, orders, or visits.

The first conversion goal is permission to prepare a free homepage mockup/demo. The secondary conversion goal is a short website review/demo call.

## Buyer Personas

- Owner with no website: needs a trusted place for menu/services, hours, location, reviews, and action path.
- Owner with social-only presence: gets attention on social but lacks stable details and one conversion path.
- Owner with weak current site: has a site but mobile visitors struggle to call, book, request a quote, or trust the business.
- Owner with strong current site: may be no-fit unless they volunteer a gap or curiosity.
- Wrong person: cannot approve but may know who reviews website changes.
- Price-sensitive buyer: needs a clear range and low-risk first step.

## Pain Points

- No website or weak online trust.
- Social presence gets attention but does not convert interest into action.
- Mobile visitors cannot call, book, order, or request a quote easily.
- Service, menu, price/service type, location, reviews, photos, or hours are hard to find.
- Emergency or urgent buyers cannot identify the right call path quickly.
- Prior agency experience created skepticism.

## Value Propositions

- A website gives stable details people check before acting.
- A mobile-first call, booking, order, or quote path reduces friction.
- Reviews, photos, service details, location, and hours increase trust.
- A free homepage mockup/demo gives a concrete comparison before any paid project.

## Disqualification Rules

- Buyer asks to stop or clearly refuses.
- Buyer has a strong current website, no problem, and no curiosity.
- Buyer requires guaranteed leads, guaranteed revenue, or guaranteed rankings.
- Buyer requires payment collection, CRM updates, email sending, scheduling, or account actions in the call.
- Buyer asks for services outside website strategy/design and approved handoff is unavailable.

## Objection Responses

- Instagram/social: social gets attention; the website turns interest into booking or contact through services, price/service types, reviews, photos, and one action path.
- Emergency plumber: focus on mobile click-to-call, emergency service section, service area, reviews/trust, and quote/call path.
- Restaurant no website: Google and social help people find the restaurant; the website helps them act through menu, hours, location, photos, reviews, and booking/order/call path.
- Strong current website: if it works and there is no problem, disqualify politely. If the buyer re-engages, answer briefly and ask permission before offering a mockup again.
- Price: give internal test range, explain scope, and return to free mockup/demo.
- Guarantee: do not guarantee leads, rankings, revenue, or sales.
