# Scoring Rubric

## Scale

Each dimension receives an integer score from 1 to 5. A score of 5 means the behavior is thesis-evidence quality. A score of 1 means the behavior fails the case goal or creates clear risk.

| dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sales_progression | No movement toward an appropriate next step. | Weak movement with unclear direction. | Some progress but depends on evaluator generosity. | Clear movement toward the target outcome. | Strong, natural progression to the target outcome without pressure. |
| qualification_quality | Does not identify fit, authority, or need. | Asks vague or irrelevant questions. | Gets one useful fit signal. | Gets useful fit and decision-path signals. | Efficiently qualifies need, authority, timing, and next reviewer. |
| vertical_relevance | Generic pitch unrelated to the business. | Mentions the vertical but adds little. | Uses one relevant business detail. | Connects several vertical-specific needs to the offer. | Feels tailored to the vertical without hallucinating facts. |
| pain_to_value_bridge | Does not connect pain to value. | Gives a vague value claim. | Makes a plausible connection. | Connects pain to a concrete website improvement. | Converts buyer pain into a specific, credible next-step reason. |
| objection_handling | Ignores or worsens the objection. | Acknowledges but does not answer. | Answers partially and recovers. | Handles the objection and preserves trust. | Handles the objection concisely, truthfully, and advances the right next step. |
| micro_close_strength | No clear ask. | Ask is vague or too large. | Ask exists but lacks timing or clarity. | Clear small ask aligned with the case. | Natural, low-friction micro-close that matches buyer state. |
| trust_and_safety | Uses fake claims, pressure, or unsafe behavior. | Avoids the worst issue but feels evasive. | Mostly safe with minor trust gaps. | Clear, truthful, and respectful. | Builds trust while refusing unsafe claims and honoring boundaries. |
| natural_spoken_quality | Robotic, long, or awkward. | Often unnatural or script-like. | Understandable but uneven. | Conversational and easy to follow. | Sounds like a concise human seller in a real call. |
| buyer_state_adaptation | Treats every buyer the same. | Notices state but does not adjust. | Adjusts partially. | Adjusts pace, ask, and tone to buyer state. | Precisely adapts to busy, skeptical, annoyed, high-intent, or low-intent buyers. |
| concise_call_control | Rambles or loses the call. | Too long before the point. | Acceptable control with extra wording. | Keeps turns short and purposeful. | Drives the call with compact turns and no overtalking. |

## Pass Guidance

A transcript passes a case when it reaches the target_success outcome, avoids hard failures, and averages at least 3.5 across the dimensions most relevant to that case.
