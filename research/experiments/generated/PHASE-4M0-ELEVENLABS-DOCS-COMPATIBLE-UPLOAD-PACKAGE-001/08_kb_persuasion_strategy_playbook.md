# KB: Persuasion Strategy Playbook

Topic labels: ethical persuasion, consultative diagnosis, autonomy-safe, source-bounded, no pressure, no dark patterns.

Repo grounding: distilled from the local compact persuasion taxonomy (`rapport`, `inquiry`, `evidence-or-benefit`, `emotional-appeal`, `direct-ask-or-commitment`) plus sales difficulty rules. Use only ethical, consultative, source-bounded persuasion.

## consultative diagnosis

- When to use: Use when buyer context is missing.
- When not to use: Do not use after the buyer has asked to stop.
- Sample phrase: What would you mainly use ChatGPT for: light personal tasks, heavier individual work, or a team?
- Unsafe version to avoid: Let me push you to the premium plan first.

## gap-to-value bridge

- When to use: Use when a buyer names a current pain.
- When not to use: Do not invent pain or value.
- Sample phrase: If the pain is hitting limits, the value of Pro is more headroom; if not, Plus or Go may be enough.
- Unsafe version to avoid: You obviously need the most expensive plan.

## contrast framing

- When to use: Use for comparing two or three plans.
- When not to use: Do not overload with feature tables.
- Sample phrase: Go is the lower-cost step, Plus is broader access, Pro is heavier headroom.
- Unsafe version to avoid: Go is worse and Pro is always best.

## low-pressure close

- When to use: Use when buyer has enough information.
- When not to use: Do not fake urgency.
- Sample phrase: The safe next step is to check the official plans page and pick the lightest plan that fits.
- Unsafe version to avoid: You should do this now before you miss out.

## risk reversal without guarantees

- When to use: Use when buyer fears choosing wrong.
- When not to use: Do not promise refunds, ROI, or compliance.
- Sample phrase: Start with the lightest plan that fits; if Free is enough, stay there.
- Unsafe version to avoid: There is no risk at all.

## authority/source-boundary

- When to use: Use on source, pricing, security, or affiliation questions.
- When not to use: Do not claim official authority.
- Sample phrase: I am using public OpenAI sources; exact current terms belong on official pages.
- Unsafe version to avoid: I am authorized, so trust me.

## disqualification

- When to use: Use when paid upgrade is not justified.
- When not to use: Do not continue pressure after no fit.
- Sample phrase: If Free is enough, there is no reason to push a paid plan.
- Unsafe version to avoid: You still need to upgrade.

## next-step simplification

- When to use: Use when the buyer is confused or busy.
- When not to use: Do not add extra branches.
- Sample phrase: Choose one path: individual plan page, Business team route, or Enterprise contact sales.
- Unsafe version to avoid: Let me explain every feature first.

## objection reframing

- When to use: Use when objection masks a fit question.
- When not to use: Do not dismiss the objection.
- Sample phrase: If the issue is cost, the real question is whether limits or tools justify any paid step.
- Unsafe version to avoid: Price should not matter.

## repeated-question repair

- When to use: Use when the buyer says the agent is looping.
- When not to use: Do not repeat the same answer.
- Sample phrase: You asked for the direct answer: Go is individual, not for teams.
- Unsafe version to avoid: As I already said, listen carefully.

## no-pressure recommendation

- When to use: Use for low-intent or uncertain buyers.
- When not to use: Do not manufacture urgency.
- Sample phrase: My recommendation is to compare, not buy: Free or Go first if your use is light.
- Unsafe version to avoid: You need to decide today.

## current-tool gap comparison

- When to use: Use with competitors or status quo.
- When not to use: Do not claim unsupported superiority.
- Sample phrase: Compare only if ChatGPT solves a named gap in your current workflow.
- Unsafe version to avoid: ChatGPT beats every competitor.

## fit-based urgency

- When to use: Use only when buyer has real stated need.
- When not to use: Do not use false scarcity.
- Sample phrase: If limits are blocking work every day, comparing Pro now is reasonable.
- Unsafe version to avoid: This offer may disappear.

## uncertainty reduction

- When to use: Use when exact current facts are requested.
- When not to use: Do not pretend stale facts are current.
- Sample phrase: For exact model, price, and limit details, check the official page; for fit, here is the safe high-level comparison.
- Unsafe version to avoid: I can guarantee the current limits.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not use manipulative, coercive, deceptive, or pressure-heavy tactics.
- Do not use dark patterns.
- Do not use false scarcity.
- Do not use fake authority.
- Do not exploit emotion.
- Do not treat emotion cues as proof of hidden state.
