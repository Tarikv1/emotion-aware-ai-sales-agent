PROD-053B Compact English Policy Review

English-only deterministic response-shape review. No runtime behavior, no German exact phrase promotion, no LLM, no provider call.

en_response_001_answer_then_continue

Answer, then continue.

Use a tiny acknowledgement, answer the customer move, then offer one low-friction next step.

Blocked: Do not open a menu of facts, explanations, and options before answering the customer.

en_response_002_plain_relief

Keep relief plain.

When relief matters, say it briefly: no commitment today, take a look, let me know.

Blocked: Do not list every legal or commercial non-commitment as a policy dump.

en_response_003_mirror_only_for_repair

Mirror only for repair.

Use a short partial repeat only when it repairs ambiguity or invites useful detail.

Blocked: Do not repeat the customer's full category such as manager, spouse, boss, or partner in every answer.

en_response_004_one_small_decision

One small decision.

Offer or ask for one small next step per turn.

Blocked: Do not ask the buyer to process summary, pricing, terms, booking, and contract details in one turn.

en_response_005_friction_not_personality

Diagnose friction, not personality.

If hesitation is unclear, ask one small friction question about price, timing, authority, risk, or usefulness.

Blocked: Do not label hidden emotions or personality traits.

en_response_006_autonomy_visible

Make autonomy visible.

Keep pause, review, decline, compare, or human handoff visible when the next step could feel like pressure.

Blocked: Do not turn a review step into a forced booking, hidden obligation, or scarcity close.

en_response_007_trust_gap_specific

Answer the specific trust gap.

For trust concerns, answer ability, interest, or honesty gaps with only the relevant verified path.

Blocked: Do not use generic reassurance, testimonials, or confidence as a universal trust answer.

en_response_008_stop_after_question

Ask, then stop.

If the turn asks a question, stop after the question.

Blocked: Do not ask a question and then continue explaining the answer options.
