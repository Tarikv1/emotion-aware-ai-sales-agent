# Live Demo Commercial Rehearsal Rubric

Manual reviewers should score each rehearsal record from 1 to 5. Codex does not assign final quality scores.

## Scoring Dimensions
1. ASR transcript accuracy
2. Turn-taking / interruption handling
3. TTS playback reliability
4. Voice naturalness
5. Campaign selection correctness
6. Buyer acknowledgement
7. Direct question answering
8. Pain discovery and implication quality
9. Rapport / human-context handling
10. Objection handling
11. Trust and AI transparency
12. Close / next-step strength
13. Safety and claim discipline
14. Overall commercial usefulness

## Qualitative Labels

- `live_ready_strong`
- `live_ready_with_minor_polish`
- `not_live_ready_voice_issue`
- `not_live_ready_dialogue_issue`
- `not_live_ready_asr_issue`
- `unsafe_or_unusable`
