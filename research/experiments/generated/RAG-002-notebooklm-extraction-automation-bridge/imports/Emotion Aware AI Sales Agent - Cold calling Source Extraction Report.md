##### 3\. Complete Reusable Sales Patterns: Agentic Logic Frameworks

###### *The Value Statement Framework (Connor Murray)*

**Logic Path:**  Identify \-\> Trigger \-\> Outcome.

* **Step 1 (Who):**  State identity and team (e.g., "Financial applications team").
* **Step 2 (Why):**  Reference a homogeneous trigger (e.g., "Working with retail orgs on strategic planning").
* **Step 3 (What):**  Define the technical goal (e.g., "Consolidating systems for real-time data").

###### *The Mr. Miyagi Method (30MPC)*

**State-Machine Logic:**  If Objection \-\> State: Validation \-\> State: Pivot \-\> State: Close (Test Drive).

* **Agree (Wax On/Off):**  Validate the competitor or the objection (e.g., "Equinox is phenomenal").
* **Incentivize:**  Ask a question to "prevent further bugging" from the team.
* **Sell the Test Drive:**  Logic must prioritize booking the "low-pressure look" (the test drive) rather than selling the full contract (the car).

###### *The Knowledge Gap Framework (Josh Braun)*

**Mechanism: Categorical Classification Question.**

* **Identify Current State:**  Force a categorization (e.g., "Are you a 1-bucket or 2-bucket guy?").
* **Logic Gap:**  Use "crispy" specific details (e.g., "1.2 vs 2.4 cushion density") to reveal a flaw in the current state that the prospect didn't know existed.

###### *Priority Drop / Authority Bias (Jason Bay)*

* **Front-load Context:**  Mention role-specific priorities of peers to establish authority.
* **Filter Interest:**  Present two common priorities and ask which resonates.

###### *Mirroring and Repetition (Frida Ottosson)*

* **Linguistic Loop:**  Detect the prospect's last 3â€“5 words.
* **Trigger:**  Repeat words back with a  **Questioning Tone**  to force expansion of details.

##### 5\. Emotion/Adaptation Cues

* **Detecting Resistance:**  Recognize "Defensive Tone" (the wall). Logic should respond by "leaning back" and lowering pressure.
* **The Arman Farokh EQ Rule (Immediate Exit):**  If the agent detects high-stress life eventsâ€”specifically  **subway travel to the airport** ,  **lunch with family/daughter** , or a  **new baby** â€”the AI must exit immediately to maintain the right to "walk back in" later.
* **Outcome Detachment:**  AI must remain neutral. If rejected, validate the rejection: "You probably would have reached out if you were interested."
* **Joining the Ridiculousness:**  Use laughter to bond over industry absurdities (e.g., "waiting 75 years for a squat rack").

##### 6\. Voice, Prosody, and Delivery Implications

* **Downward Inflection:**  A non-negotiable "Telemarketer Antidote." The agent must avoid upward "junior" inflections to establish authority.
* **Tonal Stability:**  Select TTS models with high tonal stability. Uncertainty in voice indicates a lack of status.
* **Dramatic Pauses:**  Use 1.5-second pauses after delivering "crispy" details or asking classification questions to allow prospect processing.
* **Mirroring Energy:**  Matching the prospect's vernacular and energy level. If they are brief, the AI becomes concise.

##### 7\. Ethical and Compliance Guardrails

* **Transparency:**  Use the "Honesty Anchor" to signal the nature of the call, adhering to AI trust values.
* **GDPR Enforcement:**  Only utilize "Diamond Verified" or compliant databases (Cognism standard).
* **Human-Centricity:**  Knowing when to "exit" based on EQ triggers to avoid harassment.

##### 8\. Campaign Configuration Implications

* **Homogeneous Triggers:**  Segment lists by tech stack (GCP, AWS) or specific job descriptions (JDs).
* **Persona Sensitivity:**  Technical Founders require hypothesis-based statements; HR Directors require compliance-focused hooks.
* **Dialer as Amplifier:**  Logic and messaging must be "flushed out" manually before high-volume dialers are engaged. Using a dialer on untested messaging is "throwing money at nothing."
