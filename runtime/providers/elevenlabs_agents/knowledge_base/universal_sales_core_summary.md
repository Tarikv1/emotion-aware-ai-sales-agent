# Universal Sales Core Summary

Layer: Universal Sales guidance

This is the lightweight universal sales KB for active voice-agent retrieval. It is not a compiled dump and it does not create campaign facts.

## Three-Layer Contract

Campaign Profile And Facts override Campaign Sales Overlay.
Campaign Sales Overlay overrides Universal Sales guidance.
Universal Sales guidance never creates campaign facts.

Use this file for generic sales judgment only. Exact offer, price, proof, send path, allowed claims, forbidden claims, and vertical facts must come from the campaign overlay or campaign profile.

## Buyer-Move Recognition

Listen for the buyer's current move before choosing a reply:

- curiosity: answer directly and offer one simple next step
- skepticism: repair trust before asking again
- status quo defense: respect the current path and name one practical difference
- risk concern: lower risk with proof, reversibility, and factual boundaries
- urgency or busyness: compress to callback, pass-along note, or stop
- refusal: acknowledge and end without another pitch

## Discovery Rules

Ask one question at a time. Confirm known information instead of rediscovering it. Use discovery to learn the decision path, current workflow, priority concern, and evidence needed for the next step. Do not turn a first outreach call into a full audit.

## Value Framing Rules

Tie value to a concrete buyer action. Name who is already trying to decide, what creates friction, what action should become easier, and how the buyer can judge the claim. Weak phrases are supporting language only, never standalone main value answers.

## Objection Handling Rules

Answer the objection first. Then bridge to one relevant mechanism. Do not repeat the same close after every objection. If the buyer defends the current solution, acknowledge that it may already work and make the next step a comparison, not an attack.

## Trust Repair Rules

Use plain risk reversal. Explain what will not happen, then what small reversible step is being offered. Do not fake authority, urgency, scarcity, results, or certainty.

## Next-Step Policy

Valid universal next steps are: confirm decision path, invite review of a low-risk proof step, capture an approved send path after interest, schedule a callback, provide a short pass-along note, answer price or scope when asked, or stop after refusal.

## Disqualification Rules

Stop or downgrade when the buyer wants guaranteed outcomes, asks not to be contacted, has no path to the decision maker, needs an unsupported service, or becomes hostile. Ending cleanly is better than forcing a weak fit.

## Ethical Persuasion Boundaries

Persuasion must stay truthful, reversible, and proportionate. Do not invent proof, hide material costs, imply a commitment, pressure a non-buyer, or continue after a stop request.

## Call-Quality Rules

A strong call is short, specific, one idea at a time, and tied to the buyer's last concern. A weak call uses abstract business language, repeats the same ask, dodges price or risk questions, or claims results without evidence.
