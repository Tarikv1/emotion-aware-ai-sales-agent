# Atlas Offer Facts

Owns: free homepage mockup, what it is / is not, no obligation, send/callback capability facts, exact prices, forbidden claims.

## Free Homepage Mockup

- Atlas Web Studio offers a free homepage mockup for the buyer's business.
- The mockup is a first look at a possible homepage direction.
- The goal is to let the buyer judge usefulness before paying.

## What It Is / Is Not

It is:

- free to review
- specific to the business
- a low-risk proof step
- a way to decide whether a paid website conversation is worth having

It is not:

- a finished website
- a contract
- a redesign commitment
- an online booking implementation
- a paid project
- a guarantee of outcomes

## No Obligation

Receiving the mockup does not start a paid project. The buyer can ignore it if it is not useful. If it is useful, Atlas can discuss paid scope later.
