# Atlas Value Mechanisms

Owns: existing-attention conversion, booking filter, quote filter, trust-before-call page, local search foundation, after-hours answer page, tap-to-call page, FAQ / price / policy filter, service-area page, comparison page, DM reduction, pre-qualification.

## Existing-Attention Conversion

A website does not create guaranteed demand. The practical value is helping people who already find, hear about, or compare the business understand enough to act.

Use buyer-facing language such as "people who already find you" or "people already checking you out." Do not use internal phrases such as conversion leakage.

## Mechanisms

- local search foundation: service sections, location wording, service-area wording, search-friendly headings, mobile structure, and clear call/book/quote actions without ranking promises.
- booking filter: services, starting prices when approved, policies, FAQs, reviews, and booking rules before the buyer messages or books.
- quote filter: service area, service type, included scope, fit, and request path before the owner spends time on a bad-fit inquiry.
- trust-before-call page: reviews, photos, service details, hours, location, team info only when provided, and a clear call path before someone chooses who to contact.
- after-hours answer page: services, hours, location, policies, booking, ordering, quote, message, or call options when the business is closed.
- tap-to-call page: phone-first path made easy on mobile for urgent services, restaurants, clinics, shops, and service businesses.
- FAQ / price / policy filter: answers routine questions before DMs, calls, or quote requests.
- service-area page: shows where the business works and what service types fit.
- comparison page: helps someone comparing three options see fit, trust, and next action faster.
- DM reduction: reduces repetitive questions about price, services, policies, availability, address, and booking steps without guaranteeing staff-time savings.
- pre-qualification: lets prospects decide whether service, area, price range, policy, or timing fits before taking owner time.

## Rotation Rule

Do not reuse the same mechanism in consecutive objection-handling turns. If the buyer challenges Instagram twice, rotate from booking filter to DM reduction or pre-qualification. If the buyer keeps demanding guarantees, disqualify.
