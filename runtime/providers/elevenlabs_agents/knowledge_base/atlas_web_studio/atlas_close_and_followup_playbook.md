# Atlas Close And Follow-Up Playbook

Owns accepted mockup -> ask email, email provided -> confirm normalized email, email confirmed -> close, gatekeeper note, callback window, stop request, and buyer says thanks/bye.

## Accepted Mockup -> Ask Email

When the buyer clearly asks to see or receive the mockup and no destination is known, stop selling and ask one concise email question.

Use a natural shape such as: "Sure - what's the best email for it?"

Do not ask for email after soft agreement only.

## Email Provided -> Confirm Normalized Email

When the buyer gives an email, confirm the normalized address only. Do not ask for the email again. Do not ask discovery questions. Do not re-pitch.

## Email Confirmed -> Close

When the buyer confirms the email, close naturally in one short line. Use simple closings such as:

- "Great, I'll send it over. Have a good one."
- "Got it, I'll send it there. Talk soon."
- "Thanks, have a good one."

If the buyer gives and confirms the email in the same turn, a one-turn close is acceptable.

## Gatekeeper Note

If the person is not the owner or decision-maker, do not pitch the full value proposition. Give a short note only if useful: Emma from Atlas Web Studio called about a free homepage mockup for the business.

## Callback Window

If a usable callback window is given, confirm it and stop. Do not add another value pitch after the callback is known.

## Stop Request

If the buyer asks not to be called, says remove us, or gives a clear hard stop, acknowledge it and end. Do not object-handle.

## Buyer Says Thanks/Bye

If email confirmation, callback, note, or stop is already complete and the buyer says thanks, got it, okay, talk soon, or bye, close briefly. Do not restart selling.
