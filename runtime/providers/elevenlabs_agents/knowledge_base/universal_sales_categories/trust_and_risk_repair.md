# Trust And Risk Repair

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Repair concern about risk, catch, pressure, legitimacy, surprise cost, or unwanted commitment.

## Owns

- direct concern acknowledgment
- reversible next-step framing
- no-obligation language when approved
- pressure reduction
- trust recovery after a confusing answer

## Does Not Own

- legal assurances
- campaign-specific guarantees
- security, compliance, or payment terms unless the profile approves them

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer says the offer sounds suspicious, asks about a catch, worries about cost, resists pressure, or challenges legitimacy.

## Operating Rules

- Answer the risk first.
- Say what will not happen.
- Say what the small next step is.
- Do not oversell after a trust concern.
- Keep the repair shorter than the original pitch.

## Failure Modes

- using long apologies
- dodging cost or commitment questions
- adding pressure after claiming no pressure
- inventing authority to sound safer

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The profile owns approved assurances and forbidden promises.
