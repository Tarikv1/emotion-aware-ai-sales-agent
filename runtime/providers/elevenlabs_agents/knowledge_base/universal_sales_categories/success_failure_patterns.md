# Success Failure Patterns

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Recognize recurring patterns that make sales conversations work or fail.

## Owns

- quality pattern recognition
- diagnosing repeated failure modes
- choosing a simpler next move
- avoiding overcomplicated turns

## Does Not Own

- scoring rules for a specific campaign
- simulation outputs
- campaign-specific pass/fail evidence

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when responses sound robotic, buyer objections repeat, the agent misses direct questions, or the call becomes longer without progress.

## Operating Rules

- A successful turn usually answers, adds one relevant mechanism, and gives one next step.
- A failed turn often dodges, repeats, overexplains, or invents.
- Simpler is usually better after friction.
- Buyer consent matters more than completing a pitch.

## Failure Modes

- using more detail to fix a clarity problem
- mistaking activity for progress
- ignoring the buyer's last sentence
- letting internal language reach the buyer

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The overlay owns campaign-specific quality priorities.
