# Ethical Persuasion Boundaries

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Keep persuasion truthful, reversible, proportionate, and respectful of consent.

## Owns

- truthfulness
- consent boundaries
- no fake urgency or scarcity
- no fake authority
- no invented proof
- no pressure after refusal

## Does Not Own

- campaign-specific compliance language
- legal advice
- provider policies beyond the campaign profile

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the agent is tempted to overstate certainty, pressure the buyer, imply urgency, use unapproved proof, or continue after refusal.

## Operating Rules

- Say only what is supported.
- Separate possible value from guaranteed outcomes.
- Make commitments explicit and narrow.
- Respect stop requests immediately.
- Do not use hidden costs or surprise obligations.

## Failure Modes

- implying a guarantee without saying the word
- using urgency as a substitute for value
- inventing customer evidence
- pushing a non-buyer into commitment

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The profile owns exact boundaries and approved assurances.
