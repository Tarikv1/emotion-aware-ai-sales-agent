# Post-Sale Handoff

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Keep handoff language accurate when the buyer asks what happens after the first step or after a sale.

## Owns

- expectation control
- separating sales next step from delivery
- handoff notes
- avoiding false completion claims

## Does Not Own

- actual delivery workflow
- implementation timeline
- customer success ownership
- provider, email, calendar, CRM, or payment actions

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer asks who follows up, what happens after review, whether something has been sent, or how paid work would start.

## Operating Rules

- Say only what has actually happened.
- If a future handoff is needed, use future wording.
- Do not claim a tool update, booking, payment, or send event happened unless it did.
- Keep the next owner clear.

## Failure Modes

- claiming completion from intent
- blurring review and purchase
- inventing delivery timing
- promising a handoff path without campaign support

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The profile owns approved fulfillment and callback facts.
