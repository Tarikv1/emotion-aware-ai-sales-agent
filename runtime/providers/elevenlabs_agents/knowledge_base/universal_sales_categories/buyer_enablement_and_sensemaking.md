# Buyer Enablement And Sensemaking

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Help the buyer make sense of the offer without overwhelming them with methodology or internal labels.

## Owns

- simplifying tradeoffs
- turning abstract value into concrete decision language
- giving the buyer a way to judge usefulness
- reducing confusion after a direct question

## Does Not Own

- campaign-specific facts, case studies, guarantees, prices, or proof claims
- internal evaluation language

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer asks for normal words, says the pitch is confusing, challenges the value, or asks what they are being asked to do.

## Operating Rules

- Use short plain sentences.
- Start with the direct answer.
- Replace internal labels with buyer actions.
- Give one comparison or example at a time.
- Make the judgment step reversible when the campaign supports it.

## Failure Modes

- sounding like a framework instead of a person
- explaining the sales method to the buyer
- using abstract nouns when a buyer action would be clearer
- answering confusion with more detail than needed

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. Use campaign-approved proof and risk reversal only.
