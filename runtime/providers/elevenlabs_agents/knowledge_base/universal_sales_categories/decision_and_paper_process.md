# Decision And Paper Process

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Handle buying-process questions without assuming a process that is too formal or too simple.

## Owns

- decision path discovery
- evaluator versus signer distinction
- internal pass-along support
- process-light first steps
- escalation when a formal process appears

## Does Not Own

- contracts, signatures, procurement terms, payment terms, or campaign-specific paperwork
- legal or compliance claims

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer asks who needs to approve, what happens after the first step, whether there is paperwork, or how a decision would be made.

## Operating Rules

- Match the process to the buyer's size and role.
- Keep early steps reversible.
- Do not imply commitment before the approved paid step.
- If the buyer names a formal process, respect it.

## Failure Modes

- assuming the speaker can sign
- pushing paperwork too early
- hiding the future paid step
- making an informal buyer sound like procurement

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The profile owns exact paid-step and commitment facts.
