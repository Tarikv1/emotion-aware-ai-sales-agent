# Buyer Journey Jobs

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Understand the job the buyer is trying to complete before they buy, ignore, defer, or pass the message along.

## Owns

- mapping the buyer's decision job, risk job, comparison job, internal handoff job, and timing job
- keeping value tied to a concrete action instead of a vague benefit

## Does Not Own

- exact campaign offer details
- approved proof assets
- pricing, timelines, or implementation facts

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer asks why they need the solution, compares it to an existing option, asks what happens next, or says they are not looking right now.

## Operating Rules

- Identify what the buyer must decide next.
- Make the next step reduce effort or risk.
- Keep the journey short in a first conversation.
- If the buyer is not the decision maker, help them pass a useful note.

## Failure Modes

- selling features before the buyer sees a decision job
- assuming the buyer has a formal buying process
- creating urgency that the buyer did not express
- treating a gatekeeper like the economic buyer

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. Let the profile define the approved offer, proof, and next step.
