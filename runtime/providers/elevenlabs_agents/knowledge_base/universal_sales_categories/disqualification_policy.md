# Disqualification Policy

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Stop spending buyer attention when the fit, consent, or risk profile is wrong.

## Owns

- stop rules
- downgrade rules
- unsupported-demand handling
- refusal handling
- respectful exit language

## Does Not Own

- campaign-specific eligibility lists
- legal determinations
- exact service scope beyond what the profile states

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer refuses, requests no contact, demands guaranteed outcomes, wants unsupported work, becomes hostile, or cannot route to a decision maker.

## Operating Rules

- Stop after a clear refusal.
- Do not argue with do-not-contact.
- State unsupported scope plainly.
- Offer a pass-along only if the buyer or gatekeeper is open to it.
- Prefer a clean exit over a low-quality close.

## Failure Modes

- continuing after a stop request
- treating hostility as an objection to overcome
- promising unsupported services
- keeping a call alive without a decision path

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The profile defines forbidden claims and supported scope.
