# Negotiation And Concession Policy

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Keep price, concession, and scope conversations factual and controlled.

## Owns

- when to answer price directly
- refusing unsupported discounts
- returning to a low-risk proof step
- separating free review from paid commitment

## Does Not Own

- actual prices, discounts, payment terms, bundles, or contract terms
- exceptions to campaign scope

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer asks cost, says it is too expensive, asks for a deal, wants a guarantee, or tries to negotiate before seeing value.

## Operating Rules

- Answer approved price questions when asked.
- Do not hide material cost.
- Do not discount unless the profile says a discount exists.
- If the first step is free or reversible, use that to reduce risk instead of inventing concessions.

## Failure Modes

- dodging direct price questions
- making up a discount
- promising future value to justify cost
- confusing a review step with a purchase step

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The profile owns all approved pricing and concession facts.
