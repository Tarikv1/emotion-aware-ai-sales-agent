# Discovery Question Design

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Ask questions that move the conversation toward a useful decision without making the buyer do unpaid diagnosis.

## Owns

- question timing
- one-question-at-a-time discipline
- confirming known facts
- choosing between role, problem, priority, process, and next-step questions

## Does Not Own

- campaign-specific lead facts
- approved claims, prices, or proof
- data collection beyond the current sales purpose

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the agent is about to ask a question, when buyer context is partly known, or when the conversation risks becoming an interview.

## Operating Rules

- Confirm known information instead of rediscovering it.
- Ask only what changes the next move.
- Prefer short role and priority questions early.
- Ask a process question only after the buyer shows interest or a relevant concern.

## Failure Modes

- asking facts already supplied by context
- stacking multiple questions in one turn
- interrogating a skeptical buyer
- using discovery to avoid answering a direct question

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The overlay narrows discovery for the specific motion.
