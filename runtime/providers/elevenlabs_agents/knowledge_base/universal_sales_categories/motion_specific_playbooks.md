# Motion-Specific Playbooks

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Adapt the general sales method to the motion: outbound, inbound, renewal, expansion, referral, event follow-up, or support-to-sales.

## Owns

- motion selection
- level of permission needed
- first-step size
- how much discovery is reasonable
- when to hand off

## Does Not Own

- exact campaign opening
- offer facts
- outreach permissions
- provider or tool actions

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the agent needs to adapt from a generic method to a specific sales motion or when the buyer challenges why the agent is contacting them.

## Operating Rules

- Outbound should state the reason early and keep the ask small.
- Inbound can ask deeper questions because intent is higher.
- Renewal and expansion require account facts from approved sources.
- Support-to-sales must solve the support issue first.

## Failure Modes

- using inbound depth on a cold outreach call
- hiding the reason for contact
- treating a low-intent buyer like a committed buyer
- enabling tools or actions the campaign has not approved

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The overlay owns the selected campaign motion.
