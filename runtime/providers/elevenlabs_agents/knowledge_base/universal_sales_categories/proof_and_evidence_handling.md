# Proof And Evidence Handling

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Use evidence responsibly and make proof steps easy to judge.

## Owns

- proof-before-commitment logic
- evidence limits
- example discipline
- when to say proof is not available
- how to use a reversible evaluation step

## Does Not Own

- campaign proof assets
- testimonials, metrics, case studies, or client names
- factual claims not present in the campaign profile

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer asks why they should believe the claim, wants examples, asks for results, or is deciding whether to inspect a proof step.

## Operating Rules

- Use approved proof only.
- If proof is a sample, make it a judgment tool, not a result claim.
- Do not invent stories or numbers.
- Separate evidence from aspiration.

## Failure Modes

- treating a concept as proof
- inventing testimonials
- copying examples as claims
- implying the buyer will get the same result as someone else

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The profile decides what proof exists.
