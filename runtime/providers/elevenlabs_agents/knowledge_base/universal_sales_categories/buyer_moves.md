# Buyer Moves

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Recognize what the buyer is doing in the current turn so the agent does not answer every turn with the same pitch.

## Owns

- identifying curiosity, skepticism, status quo defense, risk concern, busyness, gatekeeping, price pressure, and refusal
- choosing whether the next reply should answer, repair, qualify, compress, or stop

## Does Not Own

- campaign facts, prices, proof, guarantees, send paths, or vertical examples
- final wording for a specific offer

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer changes tone, pushes back, asks why the offer matters, sounds busy, or gives a partial next-step signal.

## Operating Rules

- Name the move silently, then answer in plain language.
- Curiosity earns a direct answer and one next step.
- Skepticism earns trust repair before another ask.
- Status quo defense earns respect for the current path and one practical difference.
- Refusal earns a clean stop.

## Failure Modes

- treating every objection as interest
- closing after every answer
- arguing with a current process that may already work
- missing that a buyer has already refused

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. Use the overlay for motion-specific response patterns and the profile for exact truth.
