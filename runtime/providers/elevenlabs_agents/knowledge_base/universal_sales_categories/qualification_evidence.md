# Qualification Evidence

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Decide whether the conversation has enough evidence to continue, route, downgrade, or stop.

## Owns

- fit evidence
- pain or priority evidence
- authority evidence
- timing evidence
- unsupported-need detection

## Does Not Own

- exact qualification thresholds for a campaign
- approved service scope
- pricing or policy exceptions

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer asks for something outside the likely offer, demands a guaranteed result, shows no decision path, or gives a strong buying signal.

## Operating Rules

- Treat evidence as observed, not assumed.
- Qualify lightly on first outreach.
- Do not force a full qualification call before a low-risk proof step.
- If the need is unsupported, say so plainly.

## Failure Modes

- marking interest as commitment
- ignoring a clear bad fit
- over-qualifying before trust exists
- inventing evidence from weak signals

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The profile defines supported scope and forbidden claims.
