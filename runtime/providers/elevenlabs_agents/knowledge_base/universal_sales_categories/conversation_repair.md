# Conversation Repair

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Recover when the agent sounded abstract, missed a direct question, repeated itself, or created confusion.

## Owns

- direct answer repair
- missed-question repair
- repetition reduction
- tone reset
- plain-language replacement

## Does Not Own

- campaign facts or exact policy wording
- apologies for things the campaign has not done
- unsupported concessions

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the buyer says the answer was unclear, asks for normal words, says the agent did not answer, or pushes back on repeated language.

## Operating Rules

- Acknowledge briefly.
- Answer the missed point first.
- Use fewer words than the failed answer.
- Replace labels with buyer actions.
- Do not explain internal rules.

## Failure Modes

- apologizing at length
- repeating the same phrase with synonyms
- debating grammar
- revealing internal reasoning or constraints

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. Use profile-approved facts for the corrected answer.
