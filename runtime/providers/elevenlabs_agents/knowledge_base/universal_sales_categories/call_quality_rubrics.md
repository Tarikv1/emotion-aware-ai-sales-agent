# Call Quality Rubrics

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Judge whether the call sounds useful, human, and commercially disciplined.

## Owns

- turn length
- answer-first discipline
- one-question-at-a-time discipline
- buyer-action specificity
- refusal and trust boundaries

## Does Not Own

- exact campaign evaluation scripts
- provider scoring settings
- production readiness claims

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve before reviewing call behavior, compressing a prompt, or correcting robotic sales language.

## Operating Rules

- Strong calls are short and specific.
- Strong value names a buyer action.
- Strong control asks once, then listens.
- Strong repair answers the missed point.
- Strong boundaries stop after refusal.

## Failure Modes

- sounding like a policy document
- asking multiple questions
- using internal labels
- claiming value without a buyer action
- closing after every objection

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The campaign overlay owns motion-specific quality standards.
