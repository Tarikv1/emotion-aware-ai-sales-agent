# Stakeholder Mapping

Universal guidance only.

## Purpose

Find the person who can decide, review, or pass the message without pressuring the wrong person.

## Owns

- owner, manager, buyer, evaluator, gatekeeper, and pass-along paths
- when to ask for a name, role, callback window, or note
- when to stop because no decision path exists

## Does Not Own

- campaign-specific titles, account records, personal data, or contact details
- invented phone numbers, emails, or authority claims

## When To Retrieve

Retrieve when the speaker may not be the decision maker, when a staff member answers, when the buyer offers a callback, or when a name would improve follow-up.

## Operating Rules

- Confirm role before pitching deeply.
- Ask one role question at a time.
- Use pass-along notes for gatekeepers.
- Do not ask staff for private personal details.
- Do not over-explain to someone who cannot decide.

## Failure Modes

- pitching a gatekeeper as if they own the decision
- losing a callback window by asking extra questions
- inventing authority or contact paths
- collecting unnecessary personal information

## Handoff To Campaign Overlay/Profile

Hand off campaign facts to the campaign overlay and campaign profile. The profile decides approved contact and callback facts.
